Loadr Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Loadr is a download manager. This policy explains what it does with your information: in short, nothing leaves your computer except the downloads you start.

What Loadr collects

Nothing. Loadr has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no server of its own. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your computer

To do its job, Loadr stores on your computer only:

  • the list of your downloads (addresses, file names, progress) so they can resume after a restart;
  • your settings (download folder, number of connections, speed limits, look, window size);
  • the files you download, in the folder you choose.

This information is kept in the app's own folder on your computer. Deleting a download from the list removes its entry.

Network connections

Loadr connects only to the servers in the links you add, to download those files. It sends them what any download needs: the request for the file and a "Loadr/<version>" user agent. Those servers' own privacy policies apply to what they receive. If your computer is set up to use a proxy, Loadr uses it.

Update check

The edition downloaded from this website checks once a day whether a newer version exists, by downloading a small file from loadr.flexkit.net. The request carries nothing but what any web request does (the app's name and version as its user agent, and your IP address, which the web host may log). You can turn this off in Options → General. App Store and Microsoft Store editions are updated by their store and do not check.

Clipboard

Loadr can notice download links you copy. This is off until you turn it on in Options, and the clipboard is only read on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Browser extension

The optional Loadr Download Helper extension sends downloads from your browser to the Loadr app on the same computer. For each download it passes on the file's address, the page you were on, the suggested file name, and the cookies your browser would send to that address, so that sites that need you to be signed in still work. This information goes only to the Loadr app on your computer, which uses it for that download and sends the cookies only to the site the file comes from. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Children

Loadr collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
